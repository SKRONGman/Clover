## 2026-09-26 (later) — this weekend only; TBD kickoffs (Danny)
- **The report:** "it's already showing games for next weekend and at the wrong times" - ND @ UNC as Fri 11:00 PM. Two causes. (1) Hot Slips had always searched every upcoming game with lines (the 2026-09-19 rule); with most of Saturday kicked off, what remained was week 5. (2) CFBD sends `start_date` = 04:00 UTC on the game's date when the kickoff is not announced yet (`start_time_tbd: true`), and the page showed that stand-in as a time - on the board and in the rail too, since row 4.
- **Fix 1, option A (rulings 6):** `hotGames(lg)` = games with a table inside `weekendWindow()` (Thu-Mon, rolls forward Monday night). Status line says "this weekend"; a league with nothing left says "No College games left this weekend with lines." `ci/fixture.py` now dates its slate on the next Saturday/Sunday (`next_saturday`) so the tests sit inside one weekend on any day of the week.
- **Fix 2 (ruling 7):** `cfbd.py` carries `tbd`; `kickDate(G)` / `kickTime(G)` (`preview1.js`) give the date from the date part and "TBD" for the time; used by the board (`preview2.js`), the rail (`kickShort`), the ladder (`whenShort`), the hot ticket header, and the Date filter's day key. Hot ticket headers now carry the date (`Sat 9/26 8:00 PM`). Takes effect on the live page at the first refresh after the push (hourly).
- **Tests:** `test_healthy_run` asserts the flag rides along (Texas is TBD in the fixture); smoke checks every hot slip sits inside the weekend window and that a TBD game reads as its date + TBD (Sat 10/3, not Fri); `smoke_rail.js` accepts TBD. Cache tag `?v=20260926wk`. Known edge: a TBD game's stand-in time is Friday 11 PM CT, so if CFBD still had no time by then the page would treat it as kicked off - in practice times are set by midweek.
