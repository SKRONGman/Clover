How sure is this?

The model's score projections are never exact. This re-runs the ticket with every score nudged a few points either way. If the verdict holds up, it's solid. If it flips, the ticket is closer to a coin toss than it looks.

Lines auto-fill from DraftKings, which runs close to Underdog. If Underdog shows a different number, type it in — the line box is editable.

Move a line

How to use this. The market line is a coin flip — nobody beats it. But when you move the line in your app, the app has to guess how likely the new line is. Our "true chance" column is checked against ~100,000 real line moves. If your app pays more than the fair payout for a move, that move is a good deal. Tap Use to put it on the Card.

How many picks on the slip?

How this works. Every game with a line gets played out 20,000 times. Hot Slips tries every combination of Winner, Spread and Over/Under picks — up to three from one game — and shows the slips most likely to hit. Same-game picks are played out together, so a favorite to win plus its own spread counts for what it really is. Tap a slip to nudge any line up or down and watch the chance update. Prohibited logs a pair Underdog wouldn't let you combine — it's a notebook for now, it doesn't change the picks.
