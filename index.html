How sure is this?

The model's score projections are never exact. This re-runs the ticket with every score nudged a few points either way. If the verdict holds up, it's solid. If it flips, the ticket is closer to a coin toss than it looks.

Move a line

How to use this. The market line is a coin flip — nobody beats it. But when you move the line in your app, the app has to guess how likely the new line is. Our "true chance" column is checked against ~100,000 real line moves. If your app pays more than the fair payout for a move, that move is a good deal. Tap Use to put it on the Card.
