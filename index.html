How this works. Every game with a line is played out 20,000 times ahead of time, centered on the market line — nobody beats the line, so we don't try. Hot slips are the combinations most likely to hit, with same-game picks played out together so a favorite plus its own spread counts for what it really is. Tap any slip to nudge a line and watch the chance update. College and NFL each get their own hot slips; your own slip can mix both. N/A marks a pick type your app doesn't offer for that game and reshuffles around it — that's remembered until the game is off the board. Prohibited logs a pair your app wouldn't let you combine — it's a notebook, it doesn't change the picks.

How sure is this?

The line is never exact. This re-runs the slip with the total and the margin each nudged up to 3 points either way — 49 versions of the game. If the verdict holds, it's solid. If it flips, the slip is closer to a coin toss than it looks.

College lines auto-fill from DraftKings and NFL lines from ESPN BET — both run close to most pick'em apps. If your app shows a different number, tap the line and pick it from the list.
